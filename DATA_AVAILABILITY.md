# Data availability

Access and reuse statements below describe the source/object distinctions established on 1 November 2026. A public accession is not a blanket license for every related host or attachment. The package preserves permitted public study identifiers needed for pairing and population definitions; it makes no anonymization claim. Patients/donors, not cells or technical repeats, are the inferential units.

For GEO molecular deposits, [NCBI's policy](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/home/about/policies/) imposes no reuse/redistribution restriction of its own; submitter rights can still apply. Cite the repository, accession and originating study. Article licenses do not automatically govern separate data deposits. GDC requires attribution and no participant identification. PDC conditions and distinctions from LinkedOmics/publisher objects are in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

The [external-input identities](workflow/config/external_input_identities.tsv) identify principal unbundled objects without redistributing them. Additional per-file acquisition IDs and checksums are retained in the eligible paired ledgers. No raw dataset is mirrored. Reconstruction dependencies and exact preparation gaps are in [REPRODUCIBILITY.md](REPRODUCIBILITY.md).

Original tables retain historical relative provenance tokens. These identify evidence, not a promise that every referenced object is distributed. The clinical-context table retains the exact provenance replacement for the GSE122401 SOFT and cohort-context objects: the SOFT is obtained at the recorded GEO route/hash; the cohort-context table requires reconstruction from that SOFT and the frozen RNA/protein pairing facts. Neither object is bundled. Internal authorization, audit, source binding and serialized analysis references remain unavailable as operational records; the methods and public numerical/identity tables supply their necessary scientific facts. Missing records must not be inferred from molecular similarity or replaced by a convenient newer input.

The seven original/first-supplement implementations remain available, alongside PE2 methods/reporting and current figure/table source routes. This revision adds permitted PE2 values and diagnostics under object-specific decisions. Code availability does not grant redistribution rights to external inputs. Current source inventory and eligibility are explicit in publication/INDEX.md; redundant views are replaced only after exact scientific-row/field coverage checks. Finished deliverables remain outside the payload. Working-tree exclusions do not erase prior Git history.

## Second supplement objects and registrations

The original STQJC and first-supplement GE7BK scopes are joined by [OSF C6KE7](https://osf.io/c6ke7/), DOI 10.17605/OSF.IO/C6KE7. The exact public plan/adoption attachment and HPA roster are bundled; private registration retrieval logs, execution receipts and stateful dispatch controls are not.

Held source-label distributions and donor-specific narrative details are removed explicitly. [Background input identities](workflow/config/second_post_effect_background_inputs.tsv) preserve exact frozen object hashes and routes; a logical catalog label is not a bundled raw input. The singleton-label correction dated 8 November is retained.

It retains source disagreement, missingness, antibody validation/limitations, RNA-consistency qualifications and NOT_SEPARATELY_ANNOTATED immune/stromal compartments. Source patient identifiers are preserved under the HPA object conditions; no anonymity claim is made. Direct metadata/gene/antibody/image links and recorded access times are provided, but no images, HTML snapshots or RNA analyses are copied. Normal-tissue source labels do not establish healthy-donor status or fine epithelial lineage.

Current [figure inputs](publication/figure_inputs.tsv) and the reconstruction catalog explicitly identify held CPTAC/LinkedOmics participant views and GSE206785 donor composition. The seven external figure inputs are reconstructed scientific projections, validated by frozen typed-content identities; they are not downloadable private production views. Their historical private-view hashes are provenance only. The public adapter consumes existing qualified protein or single-cell analysis outputs under the same source-specific terms. Upstream acquisition/qualification gaps remain as documented in REPRODUCIBILITY.md; eligible-panel mode remains visibly partial. The table layout is a cell-screened eligible projection, with complete section navigation and held-row counts. No entire accepted/display container is exported. F/G remain deferred, H/I/J and K RNA excluded; no unregistered unavailable endpoint rows or historical backup are activated.
