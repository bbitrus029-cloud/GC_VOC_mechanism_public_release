# Data availability

Access and reuse statements below describe the source/object distinctions established on 1 November 2026. A public accession is not a blanket license for every related host or attachment. The package preserves permitted public study identifiers needed for pairing and population definitions; it makes no anonymization claim. Patients/donors, not cells or technical repeats, are the inferential units.

For GEO molecular deposits, [NCBI's policy](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/home/about/policies/) imposes no reuse/redistribution restriction of its own; submitter rights can still apply. Cite the repository, accession and originating study. Article licenses do not automatically govern separate data deposits. GDC requires attribution and no participant identification. PDC conditions and distinctions from LinkedOmics/publisher objects are in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

The [external-input identities](workflow/config/external_input_identities.tsv) identify principal unbundled objects without redistributing them. Additional per-file acquisition IDs and checksums are retained in the eligible paired ledgers. No raw dataset is mirrored. Reconstruction dependencies and exact preparation gaps are in [REPRODUCIBILITY.md](REPRODUCIBILITY.md).

Original tables retain historical relative provenance tokens. These identify evidence, not a promise that every referenced object is distributed. The clinical-context table retains the exact provenance replacement for the GSE122401 SOFT and cohort-context objects: the SOFT is obtained at the recorded GEO route/hash; the cohort-context table requires reconstruction from that SOFT and the frozen RNA/protein pairing facts. Neither object is bundled. Internal authorization, audit, source binding and serialized analysis references remain unavailable as operational records; the methods and public numerical/identity tables supply their necessary scientific facts. Missing records must not be inferred from molecular similarity or replaced by a convenient newer input.

All seven project-authored analysis implementations and source-specific preparation functions are now supplied; see the per-module routes in [REPRODUCIBILITY.md](REPRODUCIBILITY.md). Code availability does not grant redistribution rights to its external inputs. No additional study data payload was added in this revision. Finished figure/table deliverables and five redundant display/grid/order files have been removed from the working-tree package; 44 figure-source tables and all retained complete numerical results remain. Removal from a working tree does not erase prior Git history.
