## 1. Scientific purpose and finite recommendation

The study asks whether human gastric cancer has an expression background relevant to the handling and formation of aldehydes reported in earlier volatile studies. Its literature anchor is the straight-chain saturated C4–C10 series: butanal, pentanal, hexanal, heptanal, octanal, nonanal and decanal. **None of these compounds was measured by this study.** Expression is not enzyme activity, metabolic flux, aldehyde concentration or volatile release. The proposed supplement should improve the interpretation of existing expression evidence without presenting a completed tissue-to-breath mechanism.

The useful clinical contribution is a reproducible account of which molecular differences recur, which depend on scoring or tissue context, and which remain uncertain. H can receive greater interpretive emphasis because of the existing evidence; G remains an original co-primary construct. Failure to localize a change, wide confidence intervals, contradictory members and absent measurements remain part of the contribution.
